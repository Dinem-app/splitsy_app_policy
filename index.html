Privacy Policy for Splitsy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Splitsy ("we", "our", or "us") is developed and maintained by Damiano Muzzolon. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Splitsy mobile application.

1. Information You Provide

Splitsy allows you to create expense groups, add participant names, and record shared expenses. You do not need to create an account or provide your email address or phone number to use the app.

  • Local Storage: By default, your groups, participant names, and expense records are stored locally on your device.
  • Online Shared Groups: If you choose to publish a group online, the group data (group name, participant names, and expense entries) is stored on cloud servers provided by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and associated with a unique sharing ID so other users with that ID can view and collaborate on the group.

2. Automatically Collected Information & Advertising

Splitsy is 100% free to use and displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (country/region)
  • App interaction, diagnostics, and ad performance data

Where required by law (such as in the European Economic Area and the UK), Splitsy uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized ads. You can manage or withdraw your ad consent preferences at any time from within the app or via your device's Google Ads settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Splitsy relies on trusted third-party services that have their own privacy policies governing how they process data:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase / Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete groups or expenses directly inside the app at any time, or remove all local data by uninstalling Splitsy from your device.
  • Online Group Data: Shared online groups are retained only while active and are automatically deleted from our cloud servers after a period of inactivity.

5. Children's Privacy

Splitsy is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
